# Cabinetfield

Was the cabinet measured?

A serious fault with no calibration for that cabinet and that time is refused. A nameplate is not a measurement. A measurement that passes becomes a factor from 0 to 1. Unitcommit multiplies the allowed power by that factor before it checks the numbers.

A shift of 1 MHz or more from the calibrated center cuts the factor in half. The two frequencies are in [docs/ODMR.md](docs/ODMR.md).

It does not decide which chargers are on, and it does not sign the record.
